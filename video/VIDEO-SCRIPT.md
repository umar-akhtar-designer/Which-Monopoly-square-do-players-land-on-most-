# Your 60-second video

If you're under 18, don't make a video.

Sixty seconds is about 140 spoken words. Here is one way to split them.

| Seconds | Part | About | Your words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 15 | Why it's interesting | 35 words | Who would care about the answer? Open with the question before you introduce yourself. |
| 15 to 40 | The probability highlight | 60 words | Name the model or the distribution you used, and show the one picture that explains it best. |
| 40 to 60 | Results | 45 words | What you found, and how sure you are. If you kept a locked test set, open it here. |

- While you talk, show your app, a chart or your thumbnail on screen.
- Leave the methods for the writeup.
- Your camera can stay off. Add captions if you can.
- Upload an MP4, WebM or MOV (iPhone) file of 60 seconds or less, and
  250 MB or less.
