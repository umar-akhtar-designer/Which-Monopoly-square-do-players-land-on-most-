# What the finished video says

This is the exact narration in `final-video.mp4`, word for word, with the shot
that is on screen. Read it at a normal pace and it takes about 47 seconds; the
video is 50 seconds with short pauses.

If you would rather use your own voice, record these three parts as three
separate audio files and the last part can be re-timed to fit.

---

## Shot 1 · title and guess note (0:00 to 0:12)

> Most Monopoly players have a theory about one square. I wanted a real answer:
> over a long game, where does a turn actually end? I guessed Jail, only fifty
> percent sure.

## Shot 2 · the chart (0:12 to 0:31)

> My model is one player rolling two dice and walking. Go To Jail sends them to
> Jail, Chance and Community Chest cards can move them again, and Jail costs one
> turn. I worked out the exact long-run answer, then checked it with a
> simulation. The bars and the red line should sit on top of each other.

## Shot 3 · the table, the hidden test, and how sure am I (0:31 to 0:50)

> Jail wins by a mile: about nine percent of turns. But it is not a property.
> The best property is Illinois Avenue, at about three percent. I tested the
> model on two hundred thousand hidden turns, and the average gap was two
> hundredths of a point. I left out doubles.
