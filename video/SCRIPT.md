# The 60-second video: final script

Read this at a normal talking speed. It is about 145 words, which is 60
seconds. If it runs long, cut the last sentence of the results part.

Show the website on screen while you talk: start on the title and the guess
note, move to the chart for the highlight, then scroll to the top-five table
for the results. Your camera can stay off. Add captions if you can.

If you are under 18, do not make the video. You still get the gold star.

---

## 0:00–0:15 · Why it is interesting (35 words)

Every Monopoly group has a theory about one square. I wanted to check one
properly: over a long game, where do players actually end up? I guessed Jail,
only fifty percent sure.

## 0:15–0:40 · The probability highlight (60 words)

My model is one player rolling two dice and walking. Go To Jail sends them to
Jail, Chance and Community Chest cards can move them again, and Jail costs one
turn. I worked out the exact long-run answer by multiplying the one-turn
chances, then checked it with a simulation. The bars and the red line should
sit on top of each other.

## 0:40–1:00 · Results (50 words)

Jail wins by a mile: about nine percent of turns. But it is not a property. The
best property is Illinois Avenue, at about three percent. I tested the model on
two hundred thousand hidden turns, and the average gap was two hundredths of a
point. I left out doubles.
